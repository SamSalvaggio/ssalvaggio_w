Samuel Salvaggio, PhD

Senior Trial Design Lead, One2Treat · Lecturer in biostatistics, ULB School of Public Health

I work on how clinical trials measure what matters: which outcomes to choose, how to prioritize them, and how to demonstrate a treatment’s value in terms patients, clinicians and regulators all recognize. I’ve worked across endpoint strategy, trial design and clinical development plans from Phase I to III, in oncology, rare neurological disease and infectious disease.
